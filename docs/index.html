Hermes Agent Remote Control

A native iPhone client for Hermes Agent

HARC drives a copy of hermes serve that you run yourself, at an address you type in. No account, and no server of ours in the middle.

Your backend is where HARC's part ends, not where your data stops: where it sends your prompts next, and what its tools may read, are its settings rather than this app's. The privacy policy has the detail.

What it does

Sessions

Streaming replies, reasoning folded away, a live plan and tool strip, tool output as cards. Pick up a session started on the desktop or in a terminal.

Approvals

When the agent asks permission, approve or deny in the app with the command in front of you, or deny from the Lock Screen. An approval you tap runs that command on your machine. Which actions stop to ask is your backend's configuration; which surfaces you get is a setting. Support has both.

Bots mode

Your backend's Hermes profiles as chats, each opening the same thread every time.

Voice chat Half-duplex

On required on-device speech recognition. Your audio stays on the phone; only the text goes to your backend.

Media and files

Images, video and reports render in the transcript; everything else opens in Quick Look. Attach photos and documents.

Desktop parity

The ten desktop themes, light and dark. Settings › Agent edits the backend's own configuration.

Notifications and Live Activities are off until you turn them on, and nothing reaches you while the app is closed.

Setup

What you need

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later, and a machine running hermes serve that the phone can reach. Binding the gateway to anything but loopback turns Hermes Agent's auth gate on, so you will need a credential for it.

The setup guide covers authentication, the serve command, the firewall, and running one backend for both clients.
